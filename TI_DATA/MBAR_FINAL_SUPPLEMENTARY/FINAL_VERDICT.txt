2. WHICH DIAGNOSTICS SUPPORT THAT SPLIT?
-------------------------------------------
Per alchemlyb's documented forward_backward_convergence procedure
(num=10, MBAR estimator), every one of the 8 systems shows the
qualitative convergence signature: forward and backward estimates
progressively narrow toward each other and toward the full-data value
as more of the trajectory is included (Figure S1 / Figure_Final).
This alone was NOT used as a pass/fail criterion, since alchemlyb
does not prescribe one.

The deciding test used the standard convention for comparing two
estimates: z = |forward - backward| / (combined uncertainty), with
z <= 2 as the ordinary 95%-confidence consistency threshold -- not a
threshold invented for this analysis. The uncertainty used was
BOOTSTRAP-CALIBRATED, not the raw analytic MBAR uncertainty, because
this analysis independently confirmed the analytic uncertainty
underestimates true estimator variability by 6-26x across all systems
tested (bootstrap vs. analytic, n=200 resamples each). Using the
tight analytic uncertainty alone would have produced spuriously large
z-scores (7-44) for nearly every system purely from an undersized
denominator, not from genuinely large absolute differences -- that
approach was rejected. Using the bootstrap-calibrated uncertainty:

    6-4PP bhd2                 z =  0.81  (CONVERGED)
    6-4PP bhd2 (docked)        z =  3.27  (BORDERLINE)
    6-4PP bhd3                 z =  0.05  (CONVERGED)
    6-4PP bhd3 (docked)        z =  3.26  (BORDERLINE)
    Lesion strand              z =  0.93  (CONVERGED)
    Lesion strand (docked)     z =  0.62  (CONVERGED)
    Partner strand             z =  0.91  (CONVERGED)
    Partner strand (docked)    z =  0.29  (CONVERGED)

6 systems land comfortably below z=1 (bhd3: 0.05; docked_lesion: 0.62;
docked_partner: 0.29*; partner: 0.91; lesion: 0.93; bhd2: 0.81).
2 systems (docked_bhd2: 3.27; docked_bhd3: 3.26) sit at z~3.3 -- outside
the 95% band even after the uncertainty correction. This is a real,
modest signal, not an analytic-uncertainty artifact.

* docked_partner's bootstrap uncertainty was still computing at generation time; a proxy (median ratio of the other 7 systems, 10x) was used for its z-score. Sensitivity check: recomputing across the full observed ratio range (5.9x-25.7x) keeps its z-score below 1.0 in every case, so this does not affect its classification.

3. IS DOCKED_BHD2 SUPPORTED AS CONVERGED AROUND -3218 kcal/mol?
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The VALUE is supported with high confidence. Five independent
analyses -- two prior non-decorrelated implementations already in
this project (ti_analysis.py: -3218.06 +/- 0.29; full_diag.py:
-3217.83 +/- 0.22) and four uniform-stride variants of the validated
pipeline used here (stride 5/10/20/40: -3219.4, -3218.1, -3224.7, -3238.2 kcal/mol)
-- all agree to within 20 kcal/mol (<0.7%) of each other. The originally
reported +5,735,757.9 kcal/mol is conclusively an artifact of an
ill-conditioned decorrelation implementation (forensic audit,
docked_bhd2_audit/) and is not the physical result.

The CONVERGENCE STATUS of the -3218 kcal/mol estimate is BORDERLINE,
not fully CONVERGED, because its forward/backward estimates disagree
by z~3.3 even under correctly-calibrated (bootstrap) uncertainty --
the same standard applied to all 8 systems. This is a materially
different, much smaller, and better-characterized issue than the
original failure: the free energy itself is not in question, but a
modest residual sampling asymmetry (visible as slower backward-curve
convergence, Figure S1) has not fully resolved within the current
10 ns/window trajectory length.

4. REMAINING CAVEATS
------------------------
  - docked_partner's bootstrap uncertainty was pending at report
    generation time (see note above); its classification is robust
    to this within the observed ratio range but is worth confirming
    with the completed value before final submission.
  - Analytic MBAR uncertainty is confirmed unreliable as a standalone
    uncertainty estimate for this dataset (6-26x too small); any
    downstream use of analytic sigma alone (e.g., in a different
    figure or table) should be flagged or replaced with bootstrap
    values.
  - docked_bhd2 and docked_bhd3, the two borderline systems, do not
    share an obvious common cause in the diagnostics collected here
    (docked_bhd2's issue was already investigated in depth via the
    forensic audit; docked_bhd3's borderline status is a new finding
    from this stricter test and has not yet been separately
    investigated).
