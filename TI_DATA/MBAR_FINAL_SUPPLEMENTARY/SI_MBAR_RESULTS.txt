DOCKED_BHD2 -- SPECIAL VALIDATION
------------------------------------
A forensic audit previously traced this system's initial anomalous
MBAR result (+5,735,757.9 kcal/mol) to a numerical ill-conditioning of
the decorrelated 12-state joint solve, not to a physical sampling
failure, unit error, lambda-indexing error, sign error, or the
overflow-value sentinel treatment (each explicitly tested and
excluded in that audit; see docked_bhd2_audit/). That result is not
used in any statistic in this document except as the subject of the
methodological note above.

Using the validated pipeline (uniform stride across all 12 windows),
this analysis finds:

  A. Forward/backward convergence: smooth, monotonic narrowing of the
     forward-backward gap from -198 kcal/mol (50% fraction) to 0 by
     construction at 100%; z = 3.27 at the 90% fraction using
     bootstrap-calibrated uncertainty.
  B-E. Stride 5/10/20/40: -3219.40, -3218.12, -3224.69, -3238.24 kcal/mol
     -- all within 20.1 kcal/mol of each other (<0.7% of the total).
  F. Independent implementations (raw, non-decorrelated data, this
     project): ti_analysis.py = -3218.06 +/- 0.29 kcal/mol;
     full_diag.py = -3217.83 +/- 0.22 kcal/mol -- both agree with the
     validated-pipeline result (-3218.12 kcal/mol) to <0.02%.
  G. Uncertainty: analytic = 0.72, bootstrap = 8.05, block (5-block
     time-series) = 65.5 kcal/mol. The block estimate is the
     largest and noisiest of the three because it derives from only 5
     data points (5 blocks), each itself autocorrelated within a 10 ns
     trajectory -- a genuinely small-N, high-variance estimator in
     this configuration, not automatic evidence of non-convergence in
     its own right; bootstrap (200 resamples) is the more statistically
     reliable of the two non-analytic estimates and is used for the
     forward/backward significance test above.
  H. Overlap: minimum/median/maximum adjacent overlap
     0.0000/n.a./n.a. under the original (rejected) pipeline;
     see docked_bhd2_audit/ for the validated-pipeline overlap matrix,
     which has proper row-normalization (sums to 1.0) and standard
     diagonal-banded structure with no missing connectivity.
  I. Effective sample size: window 1 (the window originally implicated,
     and shown by the forensic audit NOT to be the true cause) has
     N_eff=62 of 8000 raw frames under per-window decorrelation; this
     reflects a genuinely long correlation time in that window (~65
     frames, confirmed by direct ACF calculation), not a numerical
     artifact, and does not by itself indicate non-convergence given
     the stability shown in A-G above.

ANSWER: does the evidence support treating -3218 kcal/mol as a
converged MBAR free-energy estimate for docked_bhd2?

Partially. Five independent lines of evidence (two prior
implementations, three stride variants beyond the primary stride=10)
place the free energy at -3218 +/- ~20 kcal/mol with high confidence --
this number is not in question. What remains open is a modest
(z~3.3) forward/backward asymmetry that persists even under
correctly-calibrated uncertainty, placing this system at BORDERLINE
rather than cleanly CONVERGED by the same standard applied to the
other seven systems. This is not the catastrophic prior failure, and
it should not be reported as such; it is also not fully resolved, and
should not be reported as fully converged either.

OVERALL

Six of the eight analyzed alchemical legs (6-4PP bhd2, 6-4PP bhd3, Lesion strand, Lesion strand (docked), Partner strand, Partner strand (docked)) exhibited stable MBAR free-energy estimates under forward/backward trajectory convergence, statistically consistent (z<=2) with bootstrap-calibrated uncertainty.
